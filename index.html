Fuzzy Buddies

Prototype 0.2 · five species · grow and evolve

Your buddy lives on this page. Headings, the picture and the note box are all surfaces it can stand on, jump to, hang from or get knocked off of. Nothing here is scripted end to end: it picks what to do from its own moods, and the panel shows why.

Things to try

  • PokeClick it once. Poke it a few times fast and it gets grumpy.
  • PetRub your cursor back and forth over it without clicking.
  • Pick upPress on it and drag. Let go mid swing to toss it.
  • FeedDrag a word from the pantry onto it, or select any word on this page and drag it over. On a phone, tap a pantry word.
  • TypeClick the note box while it is nearby. It will clear out of your way.
  • ScrollScroll while it stands on a heading. Gentle scrolls make it wobble, hard ones knock it off.
  • WaitLeave it alone for a minute. It explores, dances, grooms and eventually naps.

The pantry

Every word your buddy eats counts toward an element: fire, water, earth, air, light or shadow. Its strongest element shapes Stage IV. Feed it plenty of a second element after that and Stage V blends the two.

Orchard notes

Moss grows on the north side of the old oak, and the bees work the clover until the fog rolls in. Embers still glow in the barn stove, where somebody left a synth humming under the thunder. There is honey on the windowsill and a riddle carved into the gate.

Select any of these words and drag them onto your buddy. It has opinions.

Leave a note

Typing is never delayed or blocked. Your buddy only reacts after the fact.

How your buddy decides

Every few seconds it scores each behavior against its current energy, curiosity, playfulness and comfort, adds a little randomness, and subtracts a penalty for anything it did recently. The winner runs until it finishes or something interrupts it: a poke, a toss, typing nearby, or the floor moving under its feet.

The same movement code handles a clean jump and a botched one. A leap can land, end in a ledge hang, or fall short, so the comedy comes from the physics instead of canned clips.

Discoveries in the panel are the first seed of progression. Each one fires a new_behavior_discovered event into the same stream that will later drive experience and evolution.
